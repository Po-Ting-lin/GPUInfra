01

NUMA stays local

The worker is CPU-affined before pinned host buffers are allocated, keeping host-side frame traffic local to its GPU.

02

No hot-path allocation

Slots and algorithm-owned output buffers are initialized and parameters are notified before frame execution; the frame path only copies and enqueues.

03

Concurrency comes from slots

A/B/C are ordered within each stream. Three streams per GPU allow three frames to remain in flight concurrently.
